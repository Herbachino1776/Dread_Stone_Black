DREAD STONE BLACK
Strategy Guide — Design-Locked Master 01–19

Stage 4E Final Assembly

Assembly date:
2026-07-03

Purpose:
This is the design-locked strategy guide master assembled from Stage 4A–4D. It applies the Stage 3 locks directly into all 19 chapters.

Core identity:
Dread Stone Black is a first-person dark fantasy RPG where the player physically clears black growth from a dying world with tools, weapons, fire, and later white-system instruments. Clearing growth reveals latches, routes, bodies, records, shrine panels, white machinery, network states, and final root connections.

Core guide rule:
The player does not collect dozens of marks, seals, chips, route tokens, or loose fragments. Progress is represented through real tools, local keys, Records, Memory Shards, map updates, route states, network states, installed components, and visible world changes.

Design locks applied:
- Maps are map updates, not carried inventory.
- Notes, ledgers, confessions, rubbings, hymns, and final memories are Records entries.
- Memory Shards live in the Memory interface, not normal inventory.
- Route progress is tracked as world-state.
- The Pale Gate Network is a network/map layer.
- Chapel Clapper upgrades the Old Procession Bell.
- Split Altar Lens and Water-Linked Lens install into their systems.
- White Interface Core I/II is treated as White Interface Level I/II upgrade progression.
- Black Sample Shard is a dangerous special object with ending risk.
- Dreadstone Avatar is the final combat boss.
- Open Anchor is the final system resolution.
- Best ending is Containment Restored.
- Quiet Folsom Return is the best epilogue variant.

GLOBAL ITEM / RECORD / ROUTE RULES
==============================================================================

Inventory should hold only what the player can equip, use, consume, install, fight with, or carry as a major dangerous object.

Records should hold readable knowledge:
- local notes
- church records
- royal records
- white memories
- black sample evidence
- final memory records

Memory Shards belong in the Memory interface.

Maps update when the player finds boards, route stones, shrine panels, white diagrams, gate traces, or restored nodes.

Route states track world changes:
- gates opened
- anchors cleared
- route points restored
- nodes restored
- echoes activated
- towers cleared
- final systems stabilized

The guide remains explicit, old-school, and practical. The actual game UI can stay restrained.

ACT I — THE VILLAGE EDGE
CHAPTER 1 — FOLSOM: THE RETURNED MAN
==============================================================================

Area Covered

Folsom Outskirts, Folsom West Gate, Folsom Common Fire, Tool Shed, Pond Path, Old Shrine Exterior, First Night Road

Main Objective

Enter Folsom, recover the first practical tools, clear black growth from the tool shed latch, restore the common fire, inspect the pond route clue, and learn that black growth is a physical covering over real things.

Tools Found

Old Work Knife  
Wood Axe  
Torch  

Map / Records / Route States

Folsom local map update  
Tool Shed Latch Revealed  
Common Fire Restored  
Pond Clue Recorded  
Old Shrine Exterior Marked  

Design note:
This chapter must teach the whole game’s basic promise. The player sees black growth, physically clears it, and reveals a usable thing underneath. Do not turn this into a named cut mechanic. Do not reward the player with a “growth shard” or route token. The reward is the opened shed, restored fire, visible latch, updated route, and changed town state.

Required Event

Wake outside Folsom, enter through the west gate, speak with the gate guard, inspect the common fire, find the tool shed, clear the black growth from the shed latch, recover the Old Work Knife, Wood Axe, and Torch, restore the common fire, inspect the pond clue, and mark the old shrine as the next route.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Wake Outside Folsom
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The game begins on the road outside Folsom. The player is facing the village wall, with pine trees behind them and the west gate ahead.

Move forward and get used to the first-person controls. Do not rush. Folsom is the starter settlement, but it should already feel wrong. The road is quiet. The pines are too still. There is black growth on the lower fence posts and old scratch marks near the gate.

Approach the west gate.

The gate guard recognizes the player but does not welcome him warmly. He says the road has been poor since the last rain and that the old shrine has started showing black marks again. He does not open with a lore speech. He gives practical warning:

Stay near the fire.  
Do not go near the shrine after dark.  
Do not touch black growth with bare hands.  

Enter Folsom.

A small map update appears:

Folsom added to map.

This is not a carried map item. It is the player’s first local map state.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Inspect the Common Fire
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

From the gate, walk toward the center of town. The common fire is ahead. It is low, smoky, and partly choked by black residue.

Speak to the villager near the fire. He says the fire used to keep the growth back. Now it gutters out at night unless someone clears the black crust from the stones.

Inspect the fire ring. Black growth covers the lower stones and blocks airflow. This is your first safe black-growth inspection, but you do not yet have the right tools.

A short route hint appears:

Find a tool before clearing the fire ring.

The villager points you toward the tool shed beside the pond path.

Do not try to make the fire ring a puzzle yet. It is a delayed payoff. The player sees the problem, then goes to find the first tools.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Find the Tool Shed
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow the path toward the pond. The tool shed is a small wooden structure near the edge of the village. It is locked, but the problem is not a normal key.

Inspect the latch.

Black growth has wrapped around the latch and hardened into a small knot. The latch is visible underneath, but it cannot move.

This is the first true black-growth clearing lesson.

The player starts with enough hand/knife interaction to cut very thin growth, or the Old Work Knife can be found wedged in the shed step, depending implementation. The cleaner route is:

Inspect the shed step.  
Take the Old Work Knife.  
Use it to cut the thin black cords around the latch.  

Equip the Old Work Knife and strike the thin cords around the latch. The growth should react in damage stages: nicked, frayed, loosened, broken.

After the cords break, scrape the black film from the latch plate and interact with the latch.

The shed opens.

Route state updated:

Tool Shed Latch Revealed.

The player should understand the rule immediately:
black growth was not the reward. The latch under it was the reward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Recover the First Tools
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Inside the tool shed, collect the Wood Axe and Torch.

The Old Work Knife remains useful for thin cords and soft growth. The Wood Axe is slower but better against hard knots, wood, and thick black crust. The Torch burns soft growth and gives light, but it does not replace physical clearing.

The shed also contains dry wood. If the current game supports wood/campfire interaction, take it. If not, treat it as environmental flavor until survival/crafting is fully ready.

Do not add unnecessary tool-shed loot. This is not a junk drawer. The chapter needs only the first tools and a clear reason to use them.

Tool roles:

Old Work Knife:
thin cords, small latches, soft body-growth.

Wood Axe:
hard knots, wooden barriers, tree chopping, thicker growth.

Torch:
soft black film, darkness, fire ring support.

Return to the common fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Restore the Common Fire
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

At the common fire, equip the Wood Axe or Old Work Knife.

The black residue around the fire ring has three parts:

Soft film over the lower stones.  
Thin cords wrapped around the air gap.  
A hard knot under the front stone.  

Use the knife or torch on the soft film. Use the knife on the cords. Use the axe on the knot.

The exact implementation can be simplified at first, but the guide logic should remain physical: clear the film, cut the cords, break the knot.

When the knot breaks, air moves through the fire ring again. Use the Torch or interact with the fire to restore it.

Route state updated:

Common Fire Restored.

NPC dialogue changes around the village. The fire villager says it burns cleaner now. The gate guard says the road may stay quiet until morning. The old shrine keeper notices the flame from near the shrine path and says the fire still remembers its work.

Do not reward a Common Fire Token. The world changed. That is the reward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Walk the Pond Path
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After the common fire is restored, follow the pond path.

The pond is quiet and useful later for fishing, but in this chapter it is mostly a clue location. The water near the far edge has black residue moving under the surface. It does not attack.

Inspect the old marker stone near the pond. The marker points toward the old shrine but is partly covered by black scab growth.

Clear the scab with the Old Work Knife or Wood Axe. The pale scratch underneath is not fully readable yet, but the route is obvious: the old shrine is connected to the pond and common fire.

Records update:

Pond Clue Recorded.

This goes into Records, not inventory. The player does not carry a Pond Mark.

If the Fishing Rod is already available in the current build, the pond can also serve as an optional survival tutorial. Do not make fishing required for Chapter 1’s main route.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Inspect the Old Shrine Exterior
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow the path from the pond toward the old shrine. Do not enter yet. Chapter 1 only marks the shrine as the next objective.

The shrine exterior has black growth around the lower stones. The door is shut, but the growth is thinner around one side wall.

Inspect the wall.

A route hint appears:

The shrine wall bears the same black growth as the common fire.

Map update:

Old Shrine Exterior marked.

The old shrine keeper warns that the inside should not be opened without more light and a better look at the growth below town.

Return to the common fire if you need safety.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Optional First Night Road
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If the player explores beyond the west gate at night, they may see the First Night Road. This is optional and should be dangerous but not mandatory.

A Wrong Dog may appear near the road edge. This enemy should be simple: a corrupted animal with black growth around the jaw and shoulder. It lunges once, recovers slowly, and can be avoided.

Do not make this a required combat test. Chapter 1 is about tools and growth clearing, not fighting.

If the player defeats or avoids the Wrong Dog, add a short record:

Something outside Folsom is carrying the same black growth as the shrine stones.

This is a Records entry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 1 Completion Checklist
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

By the end of Chapter 1, you should have entered Folsom, updated the local map, inspected the common fire, found the tool shed, taken the Old Work Knife, cleared the black growth from the tool shed latch, opened the shed, recovered the Wood Axe and Torch, restored the common fire, inspected the pond marker, recorded the pond clue, and marked the old shrine exterior as the next route.

The main skill learned in this chapter is physical black-growth clearing. You are not learning a spell or formal cut pattern. You are using tools to remove growth from real objects so the world can move again.

The main story discovery is small but important: Folsom’s fire, pond, shrine, and road are connected by black growth and older pale marks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Developer Reverse-Engineering Notes
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 1 should be the implementation testbed for the whole game.

Required spaces:
Folsom west gate, common fire, tool shed, pond path, old shrine exterior.

Required interactables:
Gate guard, common fire ring growth, tool shed latch growth, Old Work Knife, Wood Axe, Torch, pond marker scab, shrine exterior wall mark.

Required route states:
Folsom map updated, Tool Shed Latch Revealed, Common Fire Restored, Pond Clue Recorded, Old Shrine Exterior Marked.

Required enemy:
Wrong Dog optional only.

Implementation priority:
Very high. This chapter should be built before broad content expansion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 1 Core Route
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Enter Folsom through the west gate. Inspect the common fire and learn that black growth is choking it. Follow the pond path to the tool shed. Take the Old Work Knife from the shed step, cut the growth from the latch, and open the shed. Recover the Wood Axe and Torch. Return to the common fire, clear the black residue from the fire ring, and restore the flame. Walk the pond path, clear the old marker stone, record the pond clue, and inspect the old shrine exterior.

Chapter 1 ends with the first tools recovered and the old shrine marked as the next objective.

ACT I — THE VILLAGE EDGE
CHAPTER 2 — THE BLACK GROWTH BENEATH FOLSOM
==============================================================================

Area Covered

Old Shrine Exterior, Shrine Side Room, Shrine Crawlspace, Drain Channel, Three Folsom Anchors, Lower Hatch

Main Objective

Enter the old shrine side room, recover the Keeper’s Lantern and Iron Drain Bar, follow the black growth beneath Folsom, clear the three connected anchors feeding the lower hatch, and open the way into the lower shrine.

Tools Found

Keeper’s Lantern  
Iron Drain Bar  

Map / Records / Route States

Shrine Side Room Open  
Keeper’s Lantern Recovered  
Drain Channel Open  
Folsom Anchor I Cleared  
Folsom Anchor II Cleared  
Folsom Anchor III Cleared  
Lower Shrine Hatch Open  

Design note:
This chapter teaches connected growth. The player should no longer think every patch is isolated. Growth can feed a door, hatch, or blockage from nearby anchors. The reward is route opening and world-state change, not anchor fragments.

Required Event

Open the shrine side room, recover the Keeper’s Lantern, reveal hidden cords under the shrine, find the Iron Drain Bar, open the drain channel, clear the three Folsom anchors, and open the lower shrine hatch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Return to the Old Shrine
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Start from Folsom’s common fire and return to the old shrine exterior. The growth on the side wall should now be easier to read because you have tools.

Inspect the shrine side wall. Black growth covers a narrow side latch and a cracked stone joint in the masonry. Do not treat this as a formal pattern puzzle. It is a latch covered by growth.

Clear the growth from the latch. Use the Old Work Knife for thin cords and the Wood Axe for the hard knot near the lower hinge.

When the latch is exposed, interact with it. The side room opens.

Route state updated:

Shrine Side Room Open.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Search the Shrine Side Room
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The side room is small. It contains old shelves, a broken prayer board, a low table, and a lantern hanging from a peg.

Take the Keeper’s Lantern.

The Keeper’s Lantern is not just a light source. It reveals hidden growth edges, black cords, pale marks, and white-machine traces. It should become one of the player’s most important investigation tools.

Use the Keeper’s Lantern inside the side room. Black cords become visible along the floor, running under the wall toward the shrine’s lower hatch.

Inspect the low table. Add a Records entry:

Shrine Keeper’s Note.

The note says the shrine floor sometimes hums when the pond goes still. This is information, not a carried quest item.

Map update:

Shrine side room added.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Find the Shrine Crawlspace
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use the Keeper’s Lantern on the back wall of the side room. The lantern reveals black cords around a low crawlspace panel.

Clear the cords with the Old Work Knife. Break the lower knot with the Wood Axe.

Open the crawlspace panel and crouch through into the narrow space beneath the shrine.

This is the first time the player sees growth feeding through a structure rather than simply covering a latch.

Follow the cords until you reach a blocked drain channel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Recover the Iron Drain Bar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Near the drain channel is a dead maintenance worker or shrine keeper’s helper. The body is old, dry, and partly wrapped in black threads.

Do not turn this into a full enemy yet. Inspect the body with the Keeper’s Lantern. The threads show where the body was dragged toward the drain, but they are brittle.

Clear the threads from the worker’s hand and take the Iron Drain Bar.

Records update:

Drain Worker Note.

The note can be one sentence: the drain under Folsom was older than the shrine.

The Iron Drain Bar is a local pry tool. Use it on the drain grate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Open the Drain Channel
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The drain grate is stuck with rust and black growth. Use the Wood Axe to break the hard knot around the grate edge. Then use the Iron Drain Bar to pry the grate open.

Route state updated:

Drain Channel Open.

Behind the grate, the black cords split into three directions. These are the three Folsom anchors. The lower shrine hatch cannot open while they remain active.

The Keeper’s Lantern reveals the three anchor paths:

Fire Anchor, leading toward the common fire stones.  
Pond Anchor, leading under the pond path.  
Shrine Anchor, leading deeper beneath the shrine wall.  

The player can clear them in any order, but the guide uses the safest route.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Clear Folsom Anchor I — Fire Anchor
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow the left cord toward the common fire foundation. The tunnel is short and low.

The Fire Anchor is a black knot wrapped around old stones below the common fire. Because the fire was restored in Chapter 1, this knot is weaker than the others.

Use the Wood Axe to crack the outer knot. Use the Old Work Knife to cut the thin cords that remain.

When the anchor breaks, the cord running back toward the lower hatch dries and turns gray.

Route state updated:

Folsom Anchor I Cleared.

Return to the split.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Clear Folsom Anchor II — Pond Anchor
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow the middle cord toward the pond path. This tunnel is damp, and black roots run through the mud.

A Black-Root Crawler may appear here. This should be the first small growth enemy if combat is active. It crawls low, lunges once, then exposes its underside briefly. Strike after the lunge. If the underside knot is visible, attack it.

At the pond anchor, black roots wrap around a pale stone buried in the mud. Use the Keeper’s Lantern to see the full knot.

Clear the mud-growth around the stone. Break the hard outer clump with the Wood Axe. Cut the thin cords around the pale stone with the knife.

The pond cord dries.

Route state updated:

Folsom Anchor II Cleared.

Return to the split.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Clear Folsom Anchor III — Shrine Anchor
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Follow the right cord deeper beneath the shrine wall. This anchor is the strongest because it feeds directly into the lower hatch.

The Shrine Anchor is a black root mass wrapped around the underside of a pale panel. The panel is not fully usable yet, but the material is different from normal shrine stone.

Use the Keeper’s Lantern to reveal the pale panel edges. Clear the black scab from the edges first. Then break the root knot in the center.

When the knot cracks, the panel gives a faint pale pulse. The third cord dries.

Route state updated:

Folsom Anchor III Cleared.

The tunnel shakes slightly. Return to the lower hatch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Open the Lower Shrine Hatch
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Back at the lower hatch, inspect the three cords. All should be gray and dead. If one is still black, return to the matching anchor.

Use the Iron Drain Bar to pry the hatch loose. The hatch opens downward into a colder stair.

Map update:

Lower Shrine route opened.

Do not reward the player with Fire Anchor Fragment, Pond Anchor Fragment, or Shrine Anchor Fragment. The cleared anchors are route states.

Before descending, return to the old shrine keeper if desired. He has new dialogue:

The shrine was never built deep enough to explain that stair.

Descend when ready.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 2 Completion Checklist
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

By the end of Chapter 2, you should have opened the shrine side room, recovered the Keeper’s Lantern, added the shrine note to Records, revealed the crawlspace cords, opened the crawlspace, recovered the Iron Drain Bar, pried open the drain channel, cleared the Fire Anchor, Pond Anchor, and Shrine Anchor, and opened the lower shrine hatch.

The main skill learned in this chapter is connected growth clearing. A blocked route may be fed by anchors elsewhere. Follow the roots, clear the anchors, then return to the blockage.

The main story discovery is that Folsom’s shrine was built over something older, and the black growth beneath town is feeding through old structures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Required interactables:
Shrine side latch, Keeper’s Lantern, shrine note, crawlspace panel, threaded worker body, Iron Drain Bar, drain grate, three anchors, lower hatch.

Required route states:

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 2 Core Route
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return to the old shrine, clear the side latch, and open the side room. Recover the Keeper’s Lantern and use it to reveal black cords leading into the crawlspace. Clear the crawlspace panel, enter beneath the shrine, and recover the Iron Drain Bar from the threaded worker. Pry open the drain channel and follow the three cords to the Fire Anchor, Pond Anchor, and Shrine Anchor. Clear all three anchors, return to the lower hatch, and pry it open.

Chapter 2 ends with the lower shrine route open.

ACT I — THE VILLAGE EDGE
CHAPTER 3 — THE SHRINE THAT WAS NOT A SHRINE
==============================================================================

Area Covered

Lower Shrine Stair, White-Scab Hall, Shrine Mechanism Room, Pale Panel, Buried White Chamber, Crypt Access Stair

Main Objective

Descend below Folsom’s old shrine, clear black scab from the first pale machinery, learn that the shrine was built over a white-system chamber, activate the buried mechanism, and open the stair to the First Crypt.

Tools Used

Keeper’s Lantern  
Old Work Knife  
Wood Axe  
Iron Drain Bar  
Torch, optional  

Map / Records / Route States

Lower Shrine Map update  
White Mechanism Exposed  
Pale Panel Activated  
First White Record Added  
Crypt Access Stair Open  

Design note:
This chapter introduces white machinery without over-explaining it. The player should see that the shrine is not just religious stone. It was built over a pale mechanism older than Folsom. The player clears black scab from white machinery and activates a buried system.

Required Event

Descend the lower hatch, clear the white-scab hall, reveal the pale panel, activate the shrine mechanism, add the first white-machine record, and open the crypt access stair.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Descend the Lower Shrine Stair
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go down through the hatch opened at the end of Chapter 2. The stair is narrow and colder than the shrine above.

Use the Keeper’s Lantern. It reveals black scab growth on the walls and faint pale lines underneath the stone.

At the bottom, the passage opens into the White-Scab Hall.

Map update:

Lower Shrine added.

This is a map update, not a physical item.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Clear the White-Scab Hall
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The White-Scab Hall is the first place where black growth clearly covers white machinery.

The walls look like shrine stone at first, but the Keeper’s Lantern reveals pale inlays under black scab. Several areas are covered by brittle crust.

Do not attack every wall. Focus on the growth around the main pale line running along the floor.

Clear the scab from the floor line. Use the Wood Axe on hard crust, then the Old Work Knife to scrape thinner film from the pale inlay.

When enough of the line is exposed, it lights faintly and points toward the Shrine Mechanism Room.

Route state updated:

White Mechanism Exposed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Enter the Shrine Mechanism Room
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Shrine Mechanism Room is a small chamber beneath the old shrine. It contains a central stone block, a pale wall panel, and black growth wrapped around several old supports.

The room should not look like a normal magic shrine. It should feel like human builders placed shrine stone around something they did not understand.

Inspect the central block. There is no readable human inscription. The Keeper’s Lantern reveals a pale panel behind the black scab on the rear wall.

Clear the scab from the rear wall.

First, break the hard crust around the panel edge.  
Second, cut the black cords that run into the panel slot.  
Third, scrape the thin film from the pale surface.  

When the panel is clean, it lights.

Do not give the player a White Door Chip or Shrine Mechanism Chip. The panel is the object. The reward is activation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Activate the Pale Panel
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Interact with the pale panel.

The player’s wound or body reacts briefly. The panel does not speak. It shows a short silent image:

a black object falling,  
a white ring closing,  
human hands later covering the white surface with shrine stone.  

Records update:

First White Record added.

This goes into Records -> White Memories or Records -> Local Notes, depending final UI. It is not a carried item.

After the image fades, the central block slides aside and reveals a lower chamber.

Route state updated:

Pale Panel Activated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Search the Buried White Chamber
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Enter the lower chamber. It is small, pale, and damaged. Most of it is buried by collapsed earth and shrine stone.

Use the Keeper’s Lantern to inspect the walls. Black growth is present, but it is gray and brittle near the activated panel.

At the far end is a sealed stair door covered by a root mat. This is the route into the First Crypt.

Clear the root mat. The Wood Axe works best on the thick knot at the center. Use the knife to cut the thin cords around the door edge.

Once the growth breaks, use the Iron Drain Bar to pry the stair door open.

Map update:

Crypt Access Stair opened.

The stair descends into darkness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Optional Return to Folsom
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Before entering the crypt, return to Folsom if desired.

The old shrine keeper reacts to the First White Record. He does not understand the image, but he says the shrine floor used to hum before he was born.

The common-fire villager says the fire has been steady since the lower anchors were cleared.

The gate guard warns that old crypt stairs never open for kind reasons.

These updates make Folsom feel changed without turning the town into a quest hub full of markers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 3 Completion Checklist
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

By the end of Chapter 3, you should have descended the lower shrine stair, updated the lower shrine map, cleared black scab from the white floor line, entered the Shrine Mechanism Room, exposed the pale wall panel, activated the panel, added the First White Record, opened the buried white chamber, cleared the root mat from the crypt stair door, and opened the Crypt Access Stair.

The main skill learned in this chapter is white-scab clearing. Black growth can cover pale machinery. Clearing the scab reveals a system, not a collectible chip.

The main story discovery is that Folsom’s shrine was built over older white machinery connected to the black object’s arrival.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Developer Reverse-Engineering Notes
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 3 should prototype white-machine reveal.

Required spaces:
Lower Shrine Stair, White-Scab Hall, Shrine Mechanism Room, Buried White Chamber, Crypt Access Stair.

Required interactables:
Floor-line scab, rear wall pale panel, First White Record, central block, root mat, stair door.

Required route states:
Lower Shrine Map update, White Mechanism Exposed, Pale Panel Activated, First White Record Added, Crypt Access Stair Open.

Implementation priority:
High. This proves black scab -> pale panel -> route state.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 3 Core Route
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Descend the lower shrine hatch. Use the Keeper’s Lantern to reveal pale lines under black scab. Clear the floor-line scab and follow it to the Shrine Mechanism Room. Clear the rear wall panel, activate it, and receive the First White Record. Enter the buried white chamber, clear the root mat from the stair door, pry it open with the Iron Drain Bar, and descend toward the First Crypt.

Chapter 3 ends with the shrine’s true nature revealed and the crypt route open.

ACT I — THE VILLAGE EDGE
CHAPTER 4 — THE FIRST CRYPT
==============================================================================

Area Covered

Crypt Access Stair, Lower Shrine Archway, Keeper’s Store, Crypt Antechamber, Old Soldier Hall, Bone Sluice, Locked Chapel, Corpse Door, First War Carving, Root Altar

Main Objective

Enter the First Crypt, learn corpse-thread/body-growth behavior, recover the old crypt records, open the locked chapel, clear the Corpse Door, read the First War Carving, defeat the Root-Taken Knight, and open the route toward the North Gate.

Tools / Weapons Found

Old Soldier Shield  
Rusted Crypt Key  
Iron Chapel Key  

Records / Map / Route States

Crypt Map update  
Dead Knight Journal I added  
First War Rubbing added  
Corpse Door Cleared  
Root Altar Cleared  
Road Warden Proof Accepted  

Design note:
Chapter 4 introduces body-growth. Black growth can move through corpses, armor, old bones, and altar roots. The player still clears it physically, but now the growth is using dead things, not only covering doors.

Required Event

Enter the crypt, update the crypt map, find the Rusted Crypt Key, open the Old Soldier Hall, recover the Old Soldier Shield, open the Locked Chapel, clear the Corpse Door, record the First War Carving, defeat the Root-Taken Knight, and recover the proof needed to open Folsom’s north gate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Enter the Crypt Access Stair
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Descend from the lower shrine into the crypt. The walls change from pale buried machinery back into human stone. The people of Folsom or an older settlement built this crypt near the white chamber without understanding what they were near.

At the first landing, use the Keeper’s Lantern. Black threads run through the stone joints and into old burial spaces.

At the bottom, update the map from a cracked wall board.

Map update:

First Crypt added.

No carried Crypt Map is needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Search the Keeper’s Store
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Keeper’s Store is the first side room near the crypt entrance. It contains broken shelves, oil jars, old cloth, and a locked box.

A Bone Rat may attack from the shelf pile. This enemy should be simple and small. Kill it with the knife or axe.

If the thread is not cut, the corpse may stand again or lunge harder.

After clearing the sluice, take the Iron Chapel Key from the floor niche.

7. Clear the Corpse Door
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Corpse Door is a mass of dead bodies and black thread blocking the chapel’s rear passage.

Do not turn this into a pattern puzzle. It is a grotesque physical obstruction.

Use the Keeper’s Lantern to reveal the main cords:

one cord binds the upper bodies,  
one cord binds the lower legs,  
one cord runs through the central chest mass into the door frame.  

Cut the upper and lower cords first. The bodies sag away from the door.

Then break the central chest mass with the Wood Axe and cut the black thread inside.

When the central thread breaks, the bodies fall apart and the door frame opens.

Route state updated:

Corpse Door Cleared.

A chapel record updates:

The bodies were not placed here to block the door. The growth arranged them after burial.

8. Reach the Root Altar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Root Altar is the crypt’s main chamber. It contains a stone altar, black roots running into the floor, and a knight’s body kneeling at the far side.

Do not rush forward.

Walk the outer edge of the room and inspect the root anchors around the altar. The Keeper’s Lantern reveals that the roots feed into the kneeling knight.

There are two altar anchors:
left floor root,  
right wall root.  

Clear at least one before engaging if you want an easier fight. Clearing both prevents the boss from recovering as much.

Use the Wood Axe to crack the hard root knots. Use the knife on exposed cords.

When you approach the altar, the Root-Taken Knight rises.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Major Encounter: Root-Taken Knight
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Root-Taken Knight is the first major boss. It is a dead knight moved by root-growth connected to the altar.

At the start, stay near the outer ring. The knight uses slow sword swings and shield shoves. Attack after each heavy swing.

If the altar anchors are still active, the knight can pull strength from them when staggered. This may restore some health or harden the black growth on its chest.

If you cleared the anchors before the fight, the recovery is weakened or disabled.

During the fight, watch for exposed growth:

wrist cords after shield shove,  
chest root after stagger,  
back tether when it kneels.  

Strike the exposed growth when it appears. Do not chase it blindly. Normal body damage still matters, but the growth connections control recovery.

When the back tether breaks, the knight collapses.

Inspect the altar after the fight. Recover Road Warden proof as a route record/state, not a permanent trophy if possible. This proof lets Folsom’s north gate open in Chapter 5.

Route state updated:

Road Warden Proof Accepted.

The old shrine keeper reacts to the First War Rubbing. He says the carving is older than the current shrine rites.

The common-fire villager says the fire burns lower while the crypt door is open.

These lines should be short and grounded.

12. Final Action and Ending State
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The final action depends on what the player has restored, understood, and preserved.

Stabilize the white containment line. Keep or surrender the Black Sample Shard safely. Cut only the central black invasion root.

Ending D — Open Anchor Accepted.
