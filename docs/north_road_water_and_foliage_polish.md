North Road remains 712 foliage billboards across eight stamps; the improvement comes from clustering, layered understory, glades, margins, and deliberate exclusions rather than count. One pooled contact object covers at most 128 nearby-priority tree roots. Foliage materials remain shared by sprite, alpha-cutout, depth-writing, fogged, and gently time-lit.

Scout Rill, Prayer Run, Hunter Creek, Fort Approach Drain, Hunter's Mere, Prayer Pool, and Scout Tarn retain their canonical hydrology, crossings, IDs, and fishing contracts. Folsom Reed Bank receives the same shared water lighting without redesign. Current authored budget remains 7 fishing zones, 4 animated water profiles, 1 sky mesh, and 1 primary shadow caster.

Debug controls: `timeOfDay=noon|dusk|night|dawn`, `dayCycleSpeed`, and `debug=outdoor-lighting,outdoor-shadows,foliage-grounding,forest-stamps,water-material,water-banks`. Validation is consolidated under `validate:outdoors`.
